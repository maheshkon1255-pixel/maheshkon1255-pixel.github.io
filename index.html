05 / Engineering principles
Measure the critical path.

Trace first, optimize the real bottleneck, and watch tail latency—not only averages.

Design for redelivery.

Assume retries and duplicates will happen; make business effects idempotent and recoverable.

Operate what you ship.

Dashboards, safe rollouts, runbooks, and failure modes are part of the design.
